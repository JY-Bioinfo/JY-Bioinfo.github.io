## Hello

I am **Jinyuan Guo**, a researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning** and **drug discovery**, based in Shanghai, China.

My main interests:

- **Computational antibody engineering** — structure prediction, generative design, affinity maturation, developability
- **Protein language models** — sequence representation, zero-shot variant-effect scoring, family/MSA conditioning
- **Geometric deep learning** — equivariant networks over molecular structure, inverse folding
- **Immune repertoire analysis** — BCR/TCR sequencing, epitope–paratope interactions
- **Physics-based drug design** — molecular dynamics, free-energy methods, force fields

## Featured project

**[Awesome AI Antibody Design](https://JY-Bioinfo.github.io/awesome-ai-antibody-design/)** — a verified catalogue of computational methods, software, data resources and reading for antibody engineering, with an interactive searchable site. Every entry carries a resolvable identifier and licence record.
