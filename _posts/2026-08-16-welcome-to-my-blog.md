This is my new blog — a place to collect notes, essays and reading notes on topics
I care about in **AI for drug design**.

## What I write about

- **Computational antibody engineering** — structure prediction, generative design, affinity maturation, developability
- **Protein and antibody language models**
- **Geometric deep learning** over molecular structure
- **Immune repertoire analysis** — BCR/TCR, epitope–paratope interactions
- **Physics-based drug design** — molecular dynamics, free-energy methods
