This is **JY's Log** — a place for notes and essays on topics at the intersection of
**AI and drug design**: computational antibody and peptide engineering, protein language
models, structure prediction, generative design, and the physics-based foundations
underneath.

Two maintained projects anchor the material on this site:

- **Awesome AI Antibody Design** — a verified catalogue of computational methods,
  software, data resources and reading for antibody, nanobody and TCR engineering,
  presented as an interactive lineage roadmap with a searchable catalogue.
- **Peptide Drug Design Handbook** — a self-contained interactive handbook on peptide
  drug design using GLP-1-class drugs as the main thread, available in Chinese and
  English.

Posts here are reading notes, working notes and longer essays. Where a post draws on
published work, it cites the original source. Nothing here is peer-reviewed; treat it as
a personal working log, and always check the primary literature.

Happy reading.
